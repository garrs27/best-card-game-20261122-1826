How to play

  • Goal: reduce the enemy Emberlord's life from 20 to 0 before yours falls.
  • Ember is your mana: it starts at 1 and grows by 1 every turn, up to 10. Cards cost ember to play.
  • Play: click a card in your hand, then click one of the three lanes. Press End Turn (or Enter) when done.
  • Combat: after the enemy plays, creatures in each lane fight. Swift strikes first; a surviving creature whose foe is gone hits the enemy life — unless a Guard stands in that lane.
  • Keywords: ⛨ Guard shields its lane's life — even falling, it absorbs that round's blow · ⚡ Swift strikes first · ⇑ Rally gives adjacent allies +1 attack · ✚ Mend heals you 3 · ♨ Burn deals 1 to enemy life · ❄ Frost freezes the foe in its lane · ▤ Draw draws a card.
  • Keys: 1–7 select a hand card · Enter/Space ends your turn · Esc back to title · M mute.

Original game · plays fully offline · no account, no network
